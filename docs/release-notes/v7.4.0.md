## Vulnerator 2.0 — 7.4.0

**Dashboards, free and included.** Vulnerator now comes with the Dashboards add-on in `Add-ons\Dashboards`.
It needs no license key: choose **Dashboards** in the menu.

### Dashboards
- **Severity**: open findings by CAT I-IV or by eMASS level (Very High to Very Low; Nessus Critical shows as Very High),
  split by ACAS, STIG and SCAP.
- **Top hosts**: worst hosts first (ACAS and STIG, ACAS only, or STIG and SCAP; top 10, 25 or 50).
- **Compliance** by NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5 control family: Not a Finding / (Not a Finding + Open); Not Applicable left out.
- **POA&M**: open items by age and severity, overdue, due in 30 days, expired risk acceptances - the same dates as the
  eMASS POA&M export.
- **Trend**: open vs. closed over time. History starts the day Dashboards is first used.
- **Coverage**: assets with no ACAS scan, no STIG checklist, or a scan older than 30 days.
- **STIGs**: the release each checklist was submitted with and whether it is outdated; warns when the DISA STIG library
  import is older than 90 days.
- **IAVMs**: active IAVA, IAVB and IAVT by severity, with hosts affected.
- Everything stays on your machine. Every view carries the classification marking.
- An ACAS finding that the host's newest scan no longer reports is shown as **Unverified**, not as open or closed.
- Severity Very High and submitted STIG releases are recorded from 7.4.0-beta.3 on: import older scans and checklists
  again to include them.

### Also in 7.4.0 (from the betas)
- Add-on support: an `Add-ons` folder, add-on menu pages, **Settings > Integrations** and add-on licenses for paid
  add-ons (Settings > Integrations > Licenses).
- ACAS imports keep the Nessus risk factor; checklist and SCAP imports keep the STIG release each file was submitted with.
- Add-on interface version 3 (`Vulnerator.AddOns.dll` 3.0.0). See the 7.4.0-beta.1 to beta.3 notes for details.

### License
Dashboards is free but is not part of the MIT-licensed program: it ships under its own license
(`Add-ons\Dashboards\LICENSE.txt`). To remove it, close Vulnerator and delete the `Add-ons\Dashboards` folder.
